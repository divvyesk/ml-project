## 14.6 Objective check

**Documented objective: R2 above 90%. Status: NOT MET.**

The measured test R2 of 0.7229 is below the documented objective of 0.90. The paragraphs below explain why the objective is not reachable on the eight-column schema and what would be required to reach it.

The reason is a dataset gap rather than a modelling gap. Week 4 of the project document describes a nine-column CarDekho file that includes `Present_Price`, the current showroom price of the same model. The literature scores of 90-95% quoted in Week 3 are obtained on that file, where `Present_Price` alone explains most of the resale value. The CSV actually supplied for this project is the eight-column CarDekho file, which has no such column.

Two diagnostics in the table above show where the remaining error sits. `R2_log` of 0.8881 scores the same predictions on the log scale the model is fitted on, and `R2_excl_top1pct` of 0.8590 drops the most expensive one percent of test vehicles. Both are far above the headline R2, which means the model handles the bulk of the market well and loses most of its score on a small number of luxury cars whose prices are not predictable from eight columns.

To reach R2 above 0.90 on the rupee scale the project would need:

1. `Present_Price` or an equivalent current-market-value column;
2. or engine, power, mileage and seats, as in the larger CarDekho `Car details v3` file;
3. or a restriction of scope to vehicles under about Rs 15 lakh, stated explicitly as a scope limit rather than applied silently.
